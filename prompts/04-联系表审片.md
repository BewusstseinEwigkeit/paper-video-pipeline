# 联系表审片

每轮迭代用。联系表按 3 秒一帧，每格带时间戳；太长就切成 2 到 3 张，同时附上旁白文本。

```text
You are the art director signing off a <3:45> English showcase film for a <conference> paper on <topic>. Goal from the
author: "<verbatim goal>". Open and look carefully at <part1.jpg, part2.jpg, part3.jpg> (contact sheet of the CURRENT cut,
one frame every 3 s, timestamp on each tile) and <narration.json> (the voice-over, by line).
Style is fixed: <look, fonts, colour meanings>. The paper's framework figure (<Fig. 2>) <and Fig. X> must stay.

Give a strict punch list (max 20 items), ordered by visual impact. For each: timestamp, what is wrong (composition,
hierarchy, spacing/alignment, legibility, colour, motion continuity, empty or static moments, clutter, inconsistency
between sections), and the exact fix (positions/sizes/colours/timing). Flag anything that looks cheap, template-like or
unbalanced, and any frame where nothing new happens for too long relative to the narration. Also flag any number,
label or claim on screen that disagrees with the narration. End with the 3 changes that would most raise perceived
quality. Pure review: do not run commands or modify files.
```

收到清单后逐条分三类：采纳、已解决、不采纳。编造的分数、越过红线的说法、和正文不一致的计数一律不采纳。改完只抽有改动的时间段出密集联系表复核。
