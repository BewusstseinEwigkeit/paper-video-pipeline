# 设计系统评审

定风格阶段用。附上当前的联系表、参考片截图和风格帧。

```text
You are a senior motion designer (keynote launch-film level) consulting on a <3:45> English showcase film for a
<conference> paper on <topic>. Open and look at every image attached:
- <sheet.jpg>: frames of the current cut (<describe the look>).
- <reference.jpg>: a previous film the team likes.
The author says: "<the author's feedback, verbatim>". Stack: Remotion (React, frame-driven, 30 fps, 1280x720 rendered at
1.5x)<, three.js>. Fonts must be free for commercial use (OFL etc.). Current fonts: <list>.

Deliver a concrete, implementable system (Markdown, max ~1300 words):
1. Typography: exact families (OFL/free), weights, sizes at 720p, tracking, line-height for: film title, chapter label,
   on-screen labels, big numbers, chart text, captions. Max 2-3 families.
2. Motion language: a small set of named easing curves (cubic-bezier) and spring configs, standard durations, stagger
   rules; entrance/exit patterns for text, numbers, bars/lines and figure cards; hand-offs between beats (shared-element
   moves instead of fades); camera rules for 3D shots.
3. The 8 highest-impact concrete upgrades for THIS film, ranked, each with what changes on screen and how to implement it
   in Remotion (pseudo-code fine).
4. What to avoid (things that read as cheap or "AI slide").
Pure design task: do not run commands, do not modify files.
```
