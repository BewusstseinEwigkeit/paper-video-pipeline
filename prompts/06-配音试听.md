# 配音试听

比较两条配音（换音色、换语速、加提示）或检查混音时用。

```text
Listen to every file attached with your audio capability (if you cannot, reply only "CANNOT_LISTEN").
Same text, two takes per paragraph:
- <b_take1.wav> vs <b_take2.wav>: "<paragraph name>" paragraph.
Take 2 differs by: <voice / rate / instruction>.
For each pair: which take has LESS metallic buzz / vocal fry / robotic grain, and which has MORE natural pause placement
and rhythm? Verdict (take1 / take2 / tie) per criterion with 1-2 timestamps as evidence, and an overall pick.

Then excerpts of the final mix (narration + music bed):
- <mix_0-60s.wav>: <what happens where>.
Judge: (a) is the music bed level under the voice right (too quiet / right / too loud, by how many dB)? (b) is the
music-to-voice transition at the first line smooth? (c) does the lift in chapter gaps feel musical or like pumping?
(d) any clicks, hiss, buzz, harshness? Timestamps please.
At most 10 findings, short. Do not modify files.
```
