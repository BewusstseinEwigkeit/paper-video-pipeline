# 读论文定分镜

```text
You are directing a <4-minute> showcase film for the paper attached (<paper.pdf>). Audience: reviewers and researchers who
have not read the paper. Read the whole paper, then deliver:

1. One-sentence thesis of the paper, and the 3-5 claims the film must prove, each with the exact table / figure that supports it.
2. Narration draft in sections: c (title line), b (background), p (problem), m (method), r (results), e (closing).
   Line ids start with the section letter (b1, b2, ...). About 150 words per minute in total. Name every core module of
   the framework figure in the method section. Results quote numbers exactly as printed in the paper. No weaknesses in
   the closing.
3. Shot list: for every narration line, what is on screen, which paper figure (with the region to focus on), and the
   word in the line that triggers each visual change.
4. Number list: every number that will appear on screen, with its table / figure and row / column, for checking.
Do not invent numbers or curves. Mark anything that is illustrative rather than measured.
```
